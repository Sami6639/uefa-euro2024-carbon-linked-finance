# Third-party source material

The analysis uses numerical facts and source-specific definitions transcribed from official UEFA, DEKRA/University of Bielefeld and Öko-Institut publications. Each derived evidence row retains a source URL, source identifier, date and locator where available. `data/source_catalog.json` lists the official sources. `data/source_manifest.csv` records hashes of the original research snapshots; it does not imply that those snapshot files are included in this release.

Raw PDFs, HTML pages, extracted full texts and source screenshots are intentionally omitted from the portable package. Public accessibility is not treated as a redistribution licence. Users may retrieve sources through the original providers, subject to those providers' applicable terms, with the included acquisition script. Later downloads may differ from the reviewed historical bytes.

The derived tables do not establish ownership of third-party documents, and no licence for those documents is granted by this package. Citation metadata is supplied for attribution and traceability. It contains the final manuscript's selected bibliography; unselected search candidates and publisher full texts are excluded.

Before redistributing additional source material, check permission and attribution requirements at the original provider. No third-party copyright notice should be removed or replaced by a project-level code licence. The numerical analysis can be rerun from the included processed ledgers without downloading any third-party full text.
