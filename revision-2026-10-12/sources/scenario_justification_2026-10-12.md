# Dated scenario justification

Prepared 12 October 2026 to document the scenario already present in the supplied manuscript. This is retrospective documentation, not preregistration and not evidence that inputs were selected before results were known.

The observed organiser inventory, 316,912 tCO2e, supplies a dimensional reference; the €7,925,000 announced total supplies the monetary scale. Their documentary meanings remain separate from the model’s all-in donor accounting. The published €25/t pledge motivates a numerically equal model slope cap, but no evidence establishes that derivative restriction for UEFA.

The interior target q=€18.75/t is 75% of the chosen cap. The 90% advance tests a high early commitment, and the 110% exposure cap allows 10% headroom above the monetary scale. These transparent ratios make the competing feasibility restrictions legible. They are neither estimated programme conditions nor recommendations that these values are optimal. Lamerdingen’s 90% grant-to-cost ratio does not calibrate aggregate early cash.

Relative reporting standard deviation of 10% is an illustrative stress-test input, not an empirically identified error variance. The uniform law provides an analytically tractable bounded-error benchmark; the Gaussian comparison changes distribution shape at matched mean and standard deviation. Different institutional inventories cannot be treated as repeated errors without matching their boundaries and methods. Zero financing loading and verification cost form a transparent baseline; positive costs are separate scale-matched sensitivities.

Future empirical application would require a consistently defined emissions-reporting series, an authorised all-in donor budget and cap, initial financing resources, and project-specific dated outlays, transfers and legally usable cash/credit. Public documents checked on this date do not supply that full set. Existing numerical choices are retained to preserve the author’s findings, with their hypothetical status made explicit.
