# Public cash-flow search 12 October 2026

Scope: public primary programme, recipient, regional-association and municipal material. Original public interview reporting was inspected only as supplementary firsthand timing evidence. No private records were requested and no person was contacted.

Queries included: Lamerdingen UEFA Rechnung; Lommersum UEFA Konto; UEFA Klimafonds Überweisung; UEFA Klimafonds Rechnungsdatum; UEFA Klimafonds Zwischenfinanzierung; UEFA Klimafonds Zahlung Rechnung Verein; Lamerdingen Photovoltaik Kredit; Lommersum Photovoltaik 2024 Finanzierung.

Reviewed: UEFA guidelines/FAQ, January2024/July2024/January2025/July2025 announcements, ESG report p12, FSV Lamerdingen report, FVM report and corroborating association magazine search text, Lamerdingen municipal August/September2024 and May2025 indexed reports, November2024 council-report indexed text, Deutschlandfunk May9 2024 original interview, Weilerswist municipal archive indexed text.

Result: Lamerdingen gives exact investment/grant, signed agreement and beneficiary invoice references but no matched cash dates or balances. Lommersum gives ordering (funds before invoices) and a May9 publication-date bound on receipt, not exact transfer date or complete supplier ledger. Municipal and association material adds context but no quantified unrestricted cash or committed credit available at each outlay date. Search stopped after programme sources, both named recipients and independent local/public extensions failed to supply the matched minimum dataset. The conclusion is bounded public-data unavailability, not proof the data do not exist.

Minimum sufficient dataset: same identified recipient/project; each supplier payment amount and due date; each grant transfer amount/date; initial usable own cash; dated committed credit availability and restrictions; other transfers and outflows needed to avoid treating pooled cash as ring-fenced. No inspected public source supplied all fields. Figure3 must remain hypothetical.
